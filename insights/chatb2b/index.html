The Anatomy of AI-Powered ABM

Hans Bunes, independent consultant, previously HP.

ABM has promised precision at scale for decades, and AI was supposed to change that. The reality is uneven. Most programs are stalled on ICP modeling and sales-and-marketing alignment, while the harder questions remain unresolved: where AI is useful, whether that’s classical or generative, and how to prove attributable revenue. Hans Bunes has spent almost 30 years in technology go-to-market, and for the past six years been consulting independently on ABM projects and sales enablement.
